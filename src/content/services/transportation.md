## What is it?

Transport management. FlexTyres publishes the offer as a choice rather than a fixed package: **"We
can help, or if you want to, even manage the whole transportation of your used tyres from our
warehouse to your desired destination."**

## Who is it for?

Buyers without their own freight arrangements, and buyers who have them but would rather not manage
a collection from a warehouse in another country.

## How the process works

1. You give us the destination and the container size when you request a quote
2. FlexTyres confirms what it can arrange for that route
3. Tyres are packed and loaded at the warehouse
4. Transport runs to the destination you specified

Where a shipment leaves the EU, transportation is normally arranged alongside
[export and customs handling](/services/export-and-customs/).

## What you should provide

- Destination port or address
- Container size, or van collection
- Any nominated carrier or forwarder you want used
- Delivery timing constraints

## What is publicly known about limitations

FlexTyres does not publish freight rates, transit times or a list of carriers.

Under its published purchasing terms, quoted prices do not include shipping and handling charges
unless stated in writing, and title and risk pass to the buyer when goods are delivered to the
destination port named in the accepted purchase order. The full text is in the
[purchasing terms](/legal/purchasing-terms/).

One route has published delivery terms: the
[Sprinter van delivery offer](/offers/sprinter-van-delivery/) includes delivery within seven days to
Belgium, France and the Netherlands, free within a 400 km radius.
