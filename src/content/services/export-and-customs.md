## What is it?

Export documentation and customs support. FlexTyres publishes it as a partnered service: **"If you
wish we and our partners will help you handle the customs formalities and clearance. We will also
assist you if any problems should arise."**

## Who is it for?

Buyers importing used tyres outside the European Union, and buyers moving truck tyres — where
FlexTyres' published grading carries explicit export restrictions. Low-grade truck tyres are
published as "only for export"; high-grade truck tyres as "only export to Europe".

## How the process works

1. Paperwork is prepared as part of loading — FlexTyres lists "prepare all the paperwork" in its
   standard loading sequence
2. FlexTyres and its partners assist with customs formalities and clearance
3. If a problem arises in transit or at the border, FlexTyres assists

## What you should provide

- Destination country and port
- The consignee's details exactly as they must appear on the documents
- Any import licence, pre-shipment inspection or certificate your country requires

## What is publicly known about limitations

FlexTyres' published purchasing terms are explicit that compliance responsibility sits with the
buyer:

> Buyer shall comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and ordinances related to their Country.

and

> Seller disclaims any representation or warranty that the Goods conform to laws, regulations,
> ordinances, codes, or standards, except as expressly set forth by Seller in writing.

Taxes and duties are the buyer's responsibility unless FlexTyres has agreed otherwise in writing.
Used tyre imports are regulated differently in every market and some prohibit them outright, so
check your own country's rules before ordering. The full text is in the
[purchasing terms](/legal/purchasing-terms/).
