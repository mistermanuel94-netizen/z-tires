## What is it?

Loading by press. In FlexTyres' own words: **"Our experienced workers will make sure to load the
maximum amount of tyres possible into a minibus, truck or container."**

Pressed loading is the second half of the volume problem that doubling and tripling starts. Tripling
reduces the space each tyre occupies; pressed loading removes the space between them.

## Who is it for?

Every buyer shipping by container, and buyers collecting by van or truck. The published container
capacities — approximately 1,350 tyres in a 20 ft container, approximately 2,800 in a 40 ft
container, approximately 130 and 270 for truck tyres — all assume this loading method.

## How the process works

FlexTyres describes the sequence identically on its homepage, its services page and its stock page:
*"Our professionals will perform thorough quality checks, prepare all the paperwork, pack, load and
ship your tires."*

1. Quality checks before loading
2. Paperwork prepared
3. Tyres packed — doubled or tripled if you have ordered that
4. Container loaded by press
5. Shipment released

## What you should provide

The destination, the container size, and whether the order should be doubled or tripled. If you have
a preferred forwarder or a nominated shipping line, say so when you request a quote.

## What is publicly known about limitations

FlexTyres does not publish loading capacities beyond the container figures above, and does not
publish loading times. Both are confirmed per order.
