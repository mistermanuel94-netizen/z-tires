you request a quote.

## Who is it for?

Trade buyers in Belgium, France and the Netherlands who need good-condition used car tyres in van
quantities rather than container quantities. 180 tyres is the smallest published minimum of the
three purchase routes — the alternatives are a 20 ft container at roughly 1,350 tyres and a 40 ft
container at roughly 2,800.

## How pricing works

In FlexTyres' words: *"The prices we charge for tires depend on the quality of tires you want to
import, not on sizes. We have one price for all sizes."*

## What FlexTyres does

> Our professionals will perform thorough quality checks, prepare all the paperwork, pack, load and
> deliver your tires.

The company also publishes how it keeps buyers informed during an order: *"You can watch your order
travel to you in real-time. We talk to you with regular video calls and send photos and videos to
update you at every stage of the process."*

## What is published about quality

> We guarantee the quality of our tires. Whatever level of quality we export, our professionals will
> check each tire to make sure you get what you pay for.

FlexTyres publishes a replacement arrangement for this offer: if you receive damaged tyres, it will
replace them on your next order. Note that the company's general
[purchasing terms](/legal/purchasing-terms/) set a **three-day** window from receipt of goods to
notify a defect in writing.

## How to order

Send an inquiry with the sizes and mix you need. FlexTyres states it will contact you within 24
hours, and will guide you through the process until the tyres are at your door.
